# Drawing from data

Choose the route that starts with the material you have. Each leads to examples you can edit
in a box.

## Functions and measurements

Start with [Linear Algebra](linear_algebra.md) for vectors and transforms and [Lengths](lengths.md)
for dimensions in mm or inches. [Functions Examples](functions_examples.md) draws functions as
curves. [Differentiating Functions](autodiff_examples.md) draws their slopes, and
[Gaussian Processes](gaussian_processes.md) fits a function to observations.

## Time and signals

Use [Values Over Time](interpolants_examples.md) to animate using values you set, or the
[Keyframe Editor](keyframe_editor.md) to place them on a timeline. For measurements you already
have, [Signal Examples](spectral_examples.md) shows filters and spectrograms, and
[Continuing a Gesture](temporal_memory.md) looks for a continuation in a recording.

## Point sets

If all you know is how far pairs are apart, start with
[Projecting a distance matrix](projection_examples.md). If you have coordinates, use
[Points into structure](spatial_geometry_examples.md) for regions and clusters.
[Optimal Transport](optimal_transport.md) pairs points across sets, and
[Flow Matching](flow_matching.md) learns how to move between them.

## Images and paint

Use [Imaging and Computer Vision](imaging_examples.md) to read and analyse pixels or
[Depth & CLIP](onnx.md) to estimate depth and compare images. To make a picture from lines,
start with [Paint and Pencil from FLines](paint_canvas.md) or [Watercolour and Ink](watercolor.md).
A web page shown in a box is material too: [The page as FLines](web.md#the-page-as-flines) reads
its layout with a script and draws it as outlines that follow the page as it changes.

## Geometry and solids

[Meshes and 3D Geometry](mesh_examples.md) models and edits surfaces.
[Geometry From Disk](geometry_from_disk.md) brings in a file, and [Manifold](manifold.md) cuts and
combines closed solids. Use [TetGen](tetgen.md) when the interior needs a mesh too.

## Sound and music

[Sound](sound.md) gets a first rhythm playing. [Patterns](pattern_language.md) covers how
rhythms are written, and [How to make sound in Field](sound_paths.md) helps choose what plays them.
To take a rhythm outside Field or follow a shared clock, see [Network MIDI](rtp_midi.md) and
[SMPTE timecode](smpte_timecode.md).

## Audio analysis and generation

[Field Audio AI](field_audio_ai.md) is the entry for sending a job from a box.
Start with [Beat and Pitch](audio_ai_analysis.md) for timing and pitch, or use the
[Corpus Microscope](audio_ai_corpus_microscope.md) to compare sounds across recordings.

The [chain examples](chains.md) follow one project across several of these routes.

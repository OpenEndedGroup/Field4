# Ring arrangements

The **Ring arrangements** box changes geometry independently of the colour/exposure looks.
Choose an Arrangement to apply it immediately; Save as / Store geometry creates a native key.
Geometry keys live in `%document/keys/ring-geometry`; `working geometry` restores the last
arrangement on reload. Changing geometry does not seek the movie or recall an exposure look.

- **large-ring** retains the original 5 m diameter ring and its centre.
- **small-ring** has a 0.5 m diameter, placed 0.8 m forward and 0.15 m below that centre.

**Ring size and position** exposes diameter, sector fill and centre. Sector fill is 0.97 in the
current presets: increasing it widens the screens and closes the gaps. One makes a continuous
ring; larger values overlap screens, whose light adds. The 0.97 setting matches the external
gaps to the central blank region in the current stereo movie.

Both arrangements keep the stereo video's original upright orientation. The experimental
spinning, wobbling and precessing arrangement was abandoned because it does not suit stereo
video; its source and keys are archived under `files/archive`. Its graphics-queue transform and
controls have been removed. The existing Carousel layout controls remain usable.
